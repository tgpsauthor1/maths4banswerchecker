    How to play

    1. Read the question and all four answer options.
    2. Tap an option in the goal to kick the ball. You can also drag the football onto an option.
    3. With a keyboard, use Tab to reach an option and press Enter to kick.
    4. If an answer is wrong, read the explanation. That question returns later and needs two correct answers after the most recent mistake.

    Take your time. A kick outside the targets does not count as a wrong answer.
